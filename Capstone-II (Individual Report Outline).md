**Capstone-II (Individual Report Outline)**

1. **Chapter 5: Implementation**  
   1. *Overview:* Provide a brief overview of the implementation phase of your project. This should include the objectives of this phase and its importance in the overall project.  
   2. *Development Environment:*   
      1. *Hardware:* Describe the hardware used during the implementation, including specifications and purposes.  
      2. *Software:* List and describe the software tools, programming languages, libraries, and frameworks used.  
   3. *System Architecture:* Provide a detailed explanation of the system architecture. Include diagrams such as the system architecture diagram, flowcharts, or UML diagrams that illustrate how different components interact.  
   4. *Implementation Details:*  
      1. *Modules Implemented:* Describe each module implemented in detail, including its functionality and purpose.  
      2. *Algorithms/Techniques Used:* Discuss any algorithms, techniques, or methodologies applied during the implementation.  
      3. *Challenges Faced:* Highlight any technical issues or challenges encountered during the implementation and how they were resolved.  
   5. *Code Explanation:*  
      1. Provide snippets of key pieces of code and explain their function within the project.

2. **Chapter 6: System Testing**  
   1. *Overview of Testing Process:* Briefly describe the testing process, its objectives, and its significance in ensuring the project meets its requirements.  
   2. *Testing Environment:*   
      1. *Hardware:* List the hardware used during testing.  
      2. *Software:* Describe the software environment, including any testing tools or frameworks used.  
   3. *Test Cases:*   
      1. *Test Scenarios:* Outline the scenarios tested, why they were chosen, and what they aim to verify.  
      2. *Test Cases Description:* Provide detailed descriptions of individual test cases, including:  
         1. Objective: What is the test case designed to verify?  
         2. Input: What inputs are provided for this test case?  
         3. Expected Outcome: What is the expected result?  
         4. Actual Outcome: Document the actual results and whether they matched expectations.  
   4. *Test Results:*  
      1. *Summary of Test Results:* Provide a table or summary showing which tests passed and which failed.  
      2. *Discussion of Failures:* If any tests failed, discuss why and how these issues were addressed.  
   5. *Performance Testing:* Provide details of any performance tests conducted, including objectives, methodology, and results.  
   6. *Validation and Verification:* Discuss the methods used to ensure the system meets all functional and non-functional requirements.

3. **Chapter 7: Conclusion and Critical Evaluation**  
   1. *Summary of Work Done:* Recap the key elements of the project, from the objectives set out at the beginning to the implementation and testing phases.  
   2. *Critical Appraisal of the Project:*   
      1. *Strengths:* Discuss the strengths of your project, including what worked well and why.  
      2. *Weaknesses:* Reflect on the weaknesses or limitations of your project, including any aspects that did not work as expected.  
      3. *Challenges Faced:* Recap the challenges faced during the project and how you overcame them.  
   3. *Future Improvements:* Suggest any potential improvements or future work that could enhance the project, including additional features, optimizations, or alternative approaches.  
   4. *Personal Reflection:* Reflect on what you learned throughout the project. This can include technical skills, project management, teamwork, or any other relevant experiences.

***Note:** For each group project, **one hard-bound report (hard copy)** must be submitted, including all chapters from **Chapter 1 to Chapter 7**, along with properly formatted **appendix** and **reference** sections.*
